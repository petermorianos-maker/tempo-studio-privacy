Tempo Studio Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Tempo Studio is an Android music practice application. This policy explains how the app uses device permissions, local storage, and third-party advertising services.

1. Microphone and audio

Tempo Studio requests microphone access for music tuning, sound detection, and recording features. Audio is processed for these functions. Recordings may be stored on your device when you choose to save them. We do not intentionally transmit microphone recordings to an advertising service.

2. Advertising

Tempo Studio plans to use Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded advertisements. Users may choose to watch an advertisement to unlock selected premium features for 24 hours. Google and its advertising partners may process information such as device identifiers, IP address, advertising interactions, and diagnostic information, subject to applicable consent requirements.

For more information, see How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

3. Local app data

Tempo Studio may store app settings, musical preferences, and premium feature unlock expiry times locally on your device. This information helps preserve your settings and feature access between sessions.

4. Purchases

Tempo Studio plans to offer an optional one-time Pro purchase. If introduced, purchases will be processed through Google Play Billing. Tempo Studio will not directly collect payment card details.

5. Data sharing

We do not sell microphone recordings or locally stored music practice settings. Third-party services such as Google AdMob may process data as described in their own privacy policies.

6. Your choices

You can manage microphone permission through Android settings, choose whether to watch rewarded advertisements, and clear locally stored app data using Android's app settings. Where required, advertising consent choices will be offered through a consent management interface.

7. Children

Tempo Studio is a general music practice application and is not specifically directed at children. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children through the app.

8. Security and updates

We aim to use reasonable safeguards and may update this policy as features and third-party services change. Any updates will be posted on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact petermorianos@gmail.com.
